# QSMPFrontier

Server-side adventure systems for Purpur 26.1. Vanilla clients can use every
feature without installing mods.

## Survival loop

- Every new explorer receives a Frontier Compass. Right-click it to point toward
  the naturally placed battlefield.
- Crossing into the battlefield sounds war horns. Remaining in the area for the
  15-second warning starts the raid automatically; no command or operator is
  required.
- Sprint and press sneak once to dodge roll. Press swap-hands while holding a
  shield to parry.
- Craft a Tactical Whistle. Right-click air or a block to cycle Vanguard,
  Ranger, Medic, and Gatherer; right-click an owned companion to assign it.
- Craft and place Lumber, Quarry, Mine, Ranch, Greenhouse, Fishery, and Warehouse
  cores. Right-click a site with tier II or III upgrade kits to improve it.
- Production enters the site's barrel. Overflow routes to an owned or public
  Warehouse within 64 blocks instead of being thrown on the ground.
- Mine depth, Fishery water access, Greenhouse skylight, nearby timber, and a
  Gatherer companion affect production efficiency.

Administrative commands remain available for repair and testing, but none are
required for ordinary play.
